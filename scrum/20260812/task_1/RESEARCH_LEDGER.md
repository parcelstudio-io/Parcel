| [Nav2 Following Server](https://docs.nav2.org/configuration/packages/configuring-following-server.html) | It follows a supplied tracked object/frame at a desired distance and can rotate/search after loss. | Reuse its control semantics or compare against Parcel follow behavior once an identity-safe owner pose exists. | A track ID/frame is assumed; the server does not establish that the person is the owner. |
| [Nav2 Route Server](https://docs.nav2.org/configuration/packages/configuring-route-server.html) | A route graph can constrain long navigation to known edges and support route operations. | Evolve current route memory into a surveyed, policy-tagged graph for repeat city/campus routes. | It does not replace local obstacle avoidance or semantic goal grounding. |
| [Apple autonomous human following](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/human-following) | The published system combines 360-degree visual registration, face/torso re-identification, motion modeling, collision avoidance, occlusion handling, and search. | Owner following needs multimodal enrollment, temporal identity hypotheses, LiDAR association, and an explicit ambiguity/occlusion state machine. | It is research on a different platform and does not provide a drop-in open checkpoint for Parcel. |
| [GLIM](https://github.com/koide3/glim) | Open range-inertial estimation/mapping supports GPU/Jetson paths, factor-graph optimization, and ROS 2. | Include it in an L2-bag and hardware bake-off for map/odom pose and covariance. | Best choice depends on actual LiDAR, IMU, compute, motion, and timestamp quality. |
| [KISS-ICP](https://github.com/PRBonn/kiss-icp) | Open LiDAR odometry emphasizes a small, robust pipeline with ROS support. | Use as a simple CPU baseline in the same recorded-bag bake-off. | Odometry alone is not a persistent globally corrected semantic map. |
| [Elevation Mapping CuPy](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/elevation_mapping_cupy) | GPU elevation mapping includes visibility cleanup, drift compensation, traversability, and multimodal layers. | Add a 2.5-D terrain/elevation lane before authorizing ramps, hills, curbs, or stairs. | Port/integration and sensor placement require measurement on Unitree; a flat 2-D grid cannot validate terrain. |
| [ArtPlanner](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/art_planner) | Open legged-robot local planning consumes elevation/traversability maps and learned motion cost. | Evaluate as a terrain-planning challenger after elevation mapping and Sport gait limits are characterized. | It targets ANYmal/research configurations and cannot authorize Go2 foot placement without transfer evidence. |
| [ViNT/NoMaD](https://github.com/robodhruv/visualnav-transformer) | Official checkpoints/code support visual goal navigation/exploration and topological maps on wheeled robots. | Evaluate only as a shadow subgoal/candidate generator using a frozen adapter and embodiment-specific data. | The released deployment is camera/topological navigation on different robots; it is not a safe zero-shot Go2 city controller. |
| [OpenVLA](https://github.com/openvla/openvla) | Open VLA weights and fine-tuning/evaluation code are trained largely for manipulation action spaces. | Do not adopt merely because it is open; require an embodiment/action-space fit and a shadow evaluation. | Its 7-DoF manipulation actions and underlying data are a poor direct match for quadruped navigation. |
| [openpi](https://github.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi) | Open π-family checkpoints are explicitly experimental and intended to be adapted to new embodiments. | Keep as research watchlist, not P0 navigation. | Maintainers warn that transfer to different robots may not work; it does not remove data/validation needs. |
| [Gemini Robotics 1.5 design](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-15-brings-ai-agents-into-the-physical-world/) | Google's published design separates a high-level embodied-reasoning orchestrator from a lower-level VLA action model. | Supports Parcel's split between task/semantic reasoning and bounded motion candidates—not an end-to-end LLM command path. | Models are not open Unitree policies; this is architectural evidence, not a dependency recommendation. |

## Simulation and evaluation

| Primary source | Evidence used | Consequence for Parcel | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [MetaUrban repository](https://github.com/metadriverse/metaurban) and [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08725) | Compositional urban scenes include dynamic agents and micromobility tasks. | Add it as the dynamic-city perception/social-navigation lane behind a sensor-only adapter. | It is neither Go2 Sport HIL nor evidence of outdoor deployment safety. |
| [Habitat-Lab/Sim](https://aihabitat.org/) | Habitat supports modular navigation/instruction tasks, unseen scenes, and standard metrics at high simulation throughput. | Keep external ObjectNav/instruction adapters behavior-preserving and use held-out scenes to evaluate semantics. | RGB-D/GPS assumptions and discrete task definitions differ from Parcel's real sensor and continuous companion task. |
| [Habitat ObjectNav challenge definition](https://aihabitat.org/challenge/2021/) | Success and SPL are defined over unseen environments and explicit STOP. | Retain SR/SPL plus Parcel-specific terminal truth, social comfort, collisions, identity, and stop latency. | Winning an ObjectNav benchmark does not establish companion quality or physical safety. |
| [MetaDrive](https://github.com/metadriverse/metadrive) | Provides procedural scenes, dynamic traffic, point cloud/RGB/depth/semantic sensors, and fast stepping. | Useful for scalable dynamic-agent and route-policy stress, especially when MetaUrban assets/tasks are selected. | Vehicle dynamics and traffic semantics need a dog embodiment adapter; no eval may change dog behavior. |
| [NVIDIA Isaac ROS NITROS](https://nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io/concepts/nitros/index.html) | Type adaptation and negotiated GPU pipelines reduce copies between accelerated ROS nodes. | Consider only after profiling shows image/cloud transfer is a bottleneck on target Jetson hardware. | It creates NVIDIA coupling and is not needed for the first desktop/Unitree bridge. |

The evaluation rule is therefore multi-lane: keep the current fast deterministic
simulator; add dynamic urban sensor simulation; retain external frozen adapters;
add recorded sensor replay; emulate the high-level gateway; then progress to HIL
and controlled-route trials. No lane is allowed to stand in for a later rung.

## Research decisions still requiring measured evidence

1. GLIM versus KISS-ICP versus Unitree Point-LIO on the exact L2/IMU,
   including initialization, drift, relocalization, compute, and timestamp faults.
2. RPP/DWPP parameters versus MPPI on continuous dynamic scenes, with identical
   candidate-admission and collision layers.
3. Gemma 4 E4B/12B quantizations versus the frozen current model and at least one
   license-compatible challenger, under simultaneous perception/audio load.
4. Grounding/open-vocabulary detector stack on real camera sequences, including
   absent targets, signs/storefronts, lighting, blur, and calibration drift.
5. Owner enrollment/re-identification thresholds across occlusion, similar
   clothing, groups, handoff, bystanders, and explicit privacy retention limits.
6. Onboard compute, power, thermals, payload center of mass, and sensor occlusion
   after the exact robot and mount are known.

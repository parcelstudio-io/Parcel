# Adversarial review of the W1-E2 perception cutover

## A. External evidence items

**1. LLMDet (CVPR 2025 highlight) — the credible OWLv2 successor.** Open-vocab detector fine-tuned from MM-Grounding-DINO with LLM caption co-training. Zero-shot LVIS val 1.0: 34.9 / 38.5 / 42.0 AP (Swin-T/B/L). Apache-2.0 (verified in repo); checkpoints on HuggingFace, loadable via `transformers` >= 4.55. No published edge latency or VRAM figures. Relevance: it is the accuracy frontier among open-license detectors, but it is Grounding-DINO-family — Parcel's own bench measured GDINO-tiny at 106.8 ms / 2,776 MiB with fp16 crashing deformable attention (needs fp32+autocast). Its latency/VRAM shape is strictly worse than the contention-guarded OWLv2 path (86 ms, 832 MiB), and its domain-shift behavior on the LAE-80C aerial benchmark was *worse* than OWLv2 on recall (F1 0.125, recall 0.073 vs OWLv2's 0.247 over 86,558 instances).

**2. YOLOE "Real-Time Seeing Anything" (THU-MIG, 2025) and YOLO-World.** Text/visual/prompt-free open-vocab detection at YOLO speed. **AGPL-3.0** (YOLOE; YOLO-World is GPL-3.0 upstream, AGPL via the ultralytics stack Parcel's bench used). License-excluded for Parcel regardless of speed — and Parcel's bench already showed YOLO-World-S is the weakest of the three on real photos (141/156 = 90% person recall vs its 0/69 on renders).

**3. OWLv2 standing in 2026.** Still the standard robotics choice where query text embeddings are precomputed/cached and vocabulary changes per-query; Apache-2.0; NanoOWL-class TensorRT variants run at ~10 ms on Jetson (res_mapping's edge study: NanoOWL p32 fp16 9.81 ms). Its measured pathology under domain shift is false positives with large prompt lists (LAE-80C: 47,058 FP vs 21,408 TP; narrowing the prompt list from 80 to ~3 present classes yielded a 15x F1 gain) — which Parcel already mitigates with query-conditioned prompting, `multi_view_confirm`, and `false_positive_memory`.

**4. The modality gap — the mechanistic explanation for the cosine failure.** Cross-modal (text-image) cosines in CLIP/SigLIP-family spaces cluster in a narrow band far from 1.0 (mean pairwise ~0.22 in one analysis; intra-modal similarities systematically dominate cross-modal ones, producing ranking bias in mixed-modality retrieval). This is architectural, not a model-size issue — a so400m embedder widens margins but does not remove the gap. Parcel's measured spans (0.060–0.135, margins 0.0004–0.01) are the textbook signature. Consequence: absolute image-text cosine can never carry an abstention verdict; text-to-text (caption/name-based) retrieval does not have this gap. BinTrack's caption-based memory (67.4% over 270 queries vs open-source baseline 44.6%) is the working robot example, already cited in res_mapping.

**5. AgentPoison (NeurIPS 2024).** Backdoor attack on RAG/memory-augmented LLM agents (including an autonomous-driving agent) by poisoning the persistent memory store: >=80% attack success at <0.1% poison rate, as few as 2 poisoned instances, <1% benign degradation, no model access needed. Relevance: `OnlineSemanticMap` is exactly a persistent retrieval memory feeding an actuated agent, and C-2's decay-marks-never-deletes means a poisoned entry is retained *by design*.

**6. Typographic / scene-text attacks on CLIP-lineage models.** SceneTAP (2024) places scene-coherent adversarial text that misleads SOTA VLMs physically; CHAI (2025) jointly optimizes injected text signs against LVLM navigation agents with >87% attack success in real-robot experiments; household-robot typographic attack work (2026) extends this to manipulation. Relevance: W-1 explicitly adds "simple signage," and both of Parcel's perception signals — OWLv2's label head and SigLIP-2 — share the CLIP text pathway, so "detector-label agreement" is **not an independent check against scene text**: a decal reading "coffee shop" can satisfy both.

**7. Picture-of-a-person defenses.** Social-robot people-detection work: requiring RGB-detection/depth-detection bounding-box overlap (>=30%) "virtually eliminates" false humans from posters/screens; the 2026 belief-consistency line (already in res_mapping) formalizes it: VLM labels alone are insufficient for a persistent map, geometric agreement licenses trust. Cheap for Parcel: `metric_localizer` already yields metric extent, so a class-conditional size/planarity gate is nearly free.

**8. Embedding versioning practice.** Vendor and research consensus: embeddings from different models/versions occupy incompatible spaces — cosine across versions is meaningless and mixed-space ANN indexes return wrong neighbors. Standard migrations: full re-embed + swap, dual-index/named-vectors with dual writes, lazy background re-embedding; Drift-Adapter (2025) trains a small transform for near-zero-downtime upgrades. Universal best practice: store model-id + configuration metadata per vector, and retain the raw source material so re-embedding is possible. C-2's card stores persistent embeddings with none of this.

**9. Vocabulary-free naming.** CaSED (NeurIPS 2023, training-free retrieval of class names from caption databases) and 2025 vocabulary-free segmentation/fine-grained lines establish the task; open-ended detectors (GenerateU-class) generate names during detection. Reliability numbers for the VLM route: Qwen2.5-VL-7B POPE-adversarial accuracy ~82-83%; object-related caption hallucination ~5-6% (FastVLM-7B lowest at 5.81% in one 2026 comparison); Qwen3-VL semantic scene classification 86.9% (694/799 frames — aggregator-sourced). So: a VLM can name places at roughly 82-87% accuracy, i.e., ~1 in 7 names is wrong — usable only behind promotion gates, never as direct vocabulary authority.

**10. Shadow-mode evaluation limits (AV literature).** Shadow testing compares the candidate's decisions against the incumbent driver on the incumbent's trajectory; the known structural limit is distributional: the candidate is never evaluated in the states *it* would create, and compounding small errors drive agents into OOD states where prediction quality collapses (the behavior-cloning literature's core finding). High shadow agreement is therefore compatible with closed-loop failure, and low agreement is compatible with the candidate being right when the incumbent's reference is itself wrong.

## B. The five open questions, answered

**Q1 — Detector: keep OWLv2.** Every 2026 alternative fails at least one Parcel constraint: YOLOE/YOLO-World on license (AGPL/GPL), Grounding-DINO-1.5/1.6 and DINO-X on API-only access, LLMDet on latency/VRAM shape (GDINO family, no published edge numbers) — and Parcel's own bench proved the world, not the detector, is the blocker (0/69 vs 81-93% on real photos *for every detector tested*, VLM control decisive). A swap buys nothing W-1 doesn't. Name LLMDet (Apache-2.0, +3.3–14.3 AP over its MM-GDINO baseline) as the re-visit candidate **only if** the post-W-1 re-bench misses the pre-registered recall targets with textures in place.

**Q2 — Retrieval: label/text-primary, never absolute image-text cosine.** PG-3's detector-label-primary choice is correct and the modality gap explains why. Two evidence-backed additions: (i) add a *text-side* channel — name/caption entries at write time and retrieve text-to-text (lexical + text-tower cosine), the BinTrack pattern (67.4%/270 queries); this is also what makes vocabulary growth (Q3) retrievable; (ii) any residual image-text cosine is admissible only as within-query *relative* ranking with null-control calibration, never as an absence/presence verdict. A stronger embedder (SigLIP-2 so400m, ~0.9 GB fp16) is a cheap margin-widener but not a fix.

**Q3 — VLM naming of novel places: yes, bounded, and not with a resident 8B.** ~82-87% naming accuracy means wrong names *will* enter an append-only map. The workable pattern: VLM naming as an idle-time batch pass (the contention guard measured detector p95 1.54x under co-generation — never name during patrol), producing PROVISIONAL entries (`provenance: vlm_proposed`) that are promoted to admissible vocabulary only after k independent-visit re-confirmations plus the PG-3 gate. Prefer a 2-4B VLM loaded on demand over Qwen3-VL-8B resident at 19.5 GB; verify the specific small model's license before landing.

**Q4 — Embedding versioning: unaddressed in C-2 and nearly free to fix now.** Store per-entry `{embedding_model_id, revision, dim, preprocessing}` plus a bounded source-crop thumbnail; on upgrade, re-embed from stored crops (lazy or batch), never mix spaces in one index, and treat a version mismatch as "embedding unavailable" (fall back to label/text channel) rather than comparing across spaces. On a store that never deletes and persists across sessions, retrofitting this later means a schema migration plus either a full map rebuild or silently wrong retrieval.

**Q5 — Structurally missing:** see findings F1, F5, F6, F7, F8, F12 below.

## C. Findings, ranked by expected cost-to-fix-later

**F1 — The POI grounder is a second oracle that will silently invalidate C-3 and E-2. (Highest cost: a retracted eval.)** `PlaceGrounder` fires *before* semantic search (`pipeline.py:1000`), grounding any directive containing "crosswalk"/"coffee shop"/"bookstore"/"park" to hardcoded coordinates (`demo_pois.yaml`), tagged `goal_source: known_poi`. recon_groundtruth flags it explicitly ("FIRES FIRST", framing correction §4.2) — and **no card owns it**. C-3's "ONE mission driven end-to-end by T1 alone (oracle consulted by nobody)" and E-2's corpus runs are both false-positive-prone: crosswalk is one of the 8 corpus place classes, and a "pass" via the POI table measures nothing. Fix now: empty/disable the POI arm under T1 and in E-2, plus a RED seed proving the harness catches it.

**F2 — The retrieval-failure evidence is itself from the invalidated world; PG-3's operating points are calibrated against a dead distribution.** The synthesis rules "no perception number measured in this world means anything" — but applies it only to detectors. The near-chance SigLIP-2 retrieval (spans 0.060–0.135) was measured on crops of *untextured primitives* that a VLM describes as "colorful geometric shapes"; embeddings of such crops are close to degenerate. The architectural conclusion survives on external evidence (modality gap: absolute cross-modal cosine can't carry abstention), but every threshold, margin band, and agreement rate in the four-signal gate must be **re-derived on textured renders** before C-3 consumes it, and the "retrieval is the weakest link" ranking should be re-checked then. Cost if skipped: a safety gate tuned to a distribution that no longer exists — either over-refusing or, worse, admitting.

**F3 — C-2 persists embeddings with no versioning and no raw evidence.** (Q4 above.) One added schema field-set and a bounded thumbnail per entry now, versus a cross-session store migration later on a structure whose contract is "never delete." Industry practice is unanimous that cross-model cosine is meaningless; Drift-Adapter exists precisely because teams got this wrong.

**F4 — The shadow-agreement metric can be high while T1 is dangerously wrong, and low while T1 is fine.** Four concrete mechanisms: (a) *coverage bias* — the oracle drives, so T1 is never evaluated in states T1-driven behavior would create (search, scan-rotate, recovery); the AV/behavior-cloning literature says exactly this is where compounding error lives, and C-3 covers it with a single T1-only mission (n=1); (b) *structural mismatch inflation* — the oracle sees 12 m/±70° through walls with closed 9-class labels; T1 sees a ~6 m depth band with open labels and facade-based positions; divergence counts will be dominated by frustum/occlusion/convention mismatch, drowning real signal (per-class denominators help but do not separate this); (c) *no severity taxonomy* — an admission flip on a navigable wrong target (robot walks somewhere wrong) and a benign distant miss both count once; (d) *no pre-registered pass bar* — the card names the metric but no numeric criterion, inviting post-hoc threshold choice in a repo whose entire culture is pre-registration. Fix in the card, before running: pre-registered per-class thresholds; divergence classes {benign_miss, localization_delta, admission_flip, refusal_flip} with refusal/admission flips as hard gates; >=3 T1-only closed-loop missions.

**F5 — Map hygiene: dynamic objects and pictures-of-things enter the map, and all four PG-3 signals pass.** (a) C-2 has uniform decay but no class-conditional persistence: pedestrians and parked cars become object entries with growing evidence counts. DualMap's measured static-to-dynamic drop (70.5% → 64.8/60.3% HM3D; 92.3% → 69.2% real meeting room) is the price of weak volatile-object handling; res_mapping already recommended anchor/volatile persistence and the card did not adopt it. (b) A photorealistic person-poster or storefront decal defeats the four-signal gate outright: detector fires on it (agreement passes), it is re-observed every patrol (evidence *strengthens*), margins are fine, and the adjacent ground is navigable — unlike "the moon," the navigability gate does not save it. AgentPoison shows persistent-memory agents are steerable at ~2 poisoned entries; typographic attacks (>87% embodied ASR in CHAI) ride the same CLIP text pathway as both of Parcel's signals, so signage can forge label agreement. Fixes, all cheap in sim: a fifth signal — class-conditional metric size + depth-planarity consistency (RGB/depth agreement virtually eliminates picture-humans in the social-robot literature; geometry-licenses-trust per the 2026 belief-consistency line); a volatile-class exclusion list for persistence; defined quarantine semantics for decay-marked entries (marked must mean *excluded from retrieval*, not just annotated). And W-1 should add the decoys — a person poster and a scene-text decal as red-team props — because nothing in the current plan can even *exercise* the poisoning class; E-2's null controls are absent-queries only.

**F6 — Every claim in the chain is conditioned on oracle pose, and no card says so.** bench_mapping: "camera pose taken as known — this bench tests semantics, not SLAM." GT-8 is `data.qpos`; the place graph's `embed_fn` is a 64-d hash stub with VPR explicitly disclaimed. So C-2's cross-session persistence ("the dog that walked yesterday knows the lamppost today") is trivially true when MAP == truth, while the literature's actual persistent-map killers — odometry drift (VLMaps' named failure), relocalization, day/night revisit (same system 67.4% → 45.3%) — are entirely outside the test surface. The 1–3 cm fusion number will be quoted for months; it is a known-pose number. Cheap probe already in-repo: run one C-2 map-build + query cell under `DriftingOdomProvider` and report sensitivity; stamp "oracle-pose-conditioned" on the map claims and E-2's README.

**F7 — E-2, as designed, under-supports its claim and omits its own research brief's controls.** (a) One held-out scene: OVON holds out 36; n=1 licenses "an unseen scene," and the card should quantify with Wilson intervals over its 13 queries rather than point rates. (b) **No blind baseline** — res_evalgen's own item 3 ("if blind-Parcel scores within ~10 points, the perception stack is decoration"); with route-memory, priors, and any POI residue (F1), this is a live risk and it is the cheapest decisive control. (c) **No phrasing axis** — rows 1–13 are the same dev phrasings; OVON measured 11.9–23.0 points lost to phrasing alone; a synonym band generated per the OVON similarity tiers is nearly free and is the only way E-2 can detect vocabulary overfit to corpus wording. (d) **No lighting cell** — OSMa-Bench: a lighting change alone halves ConceptGraphs f-mIoU (27.9 → 14.8); MuJoCo renders one fixed lighting; one dusk/low-sun re-render of the held-out patrol is a cheap axis. (e) *Texture-pool contamination*: both scenes textured from the same CC0 library by the same author — declare the asset overlap or make the pools disjoint, else "different textures" is weaker than it sounds. (f) The shortcut probe (recolor/retexture; measured 69% SR collapse in the literature) from res_evalgen is also absent — a one-day card with outsized truth yield.

**F8 — The person channel: headline metric of W-1, consumer of nothing, and unscheduled thereafter.** W-1's acceptance is person recall >= 0.5, but under the entire W1-E2 chain no runtime consumes detector person detections — yield/keepout deliberately stay on the GT-7 oracle track (C-3 asserts it). That staging is defensible; what is missing is the explicit statement plus a follow-on card, because GT-7 is the ledger's highest-safety-value item ("9/10 to produce well enough that the existing envelopes still hold — envelopes tuned against zero-variance tracks") and the program's optics ("the dog's own vision") invite believing it is done.

**F9–F11 — the three open-question resolutions** (detector: keep OWLv2, re-visit post-W-1 with LLMDet named; retrieval: text-primary with caption channel; naming: provisional-promotion pattern with off-peak small VLM) — costs are low now, high later only if C-2/C-3 land APIs that assume absolute-cosine retrieval or unversioned embeddings.

**F12 — Small, cheap, currently unowned:** `SCENE_HONESTY_NOTE` ("the robot has no eyes") becomes actively false under T1 and instructs the hosted model to deny a real capability — recon GT-14 flagged it, no card owns it; `PerceptionContract`'s false `simulator_ground_truth: False` assertion (recon proposed it as an acceptance test) — unowned; C-2 states no bound on entry count or per-entry observation history (decay-marks-never-deletes is monotone growth by construction — fine at 108 KiB/36 places, unproven at weeks of pedestrian sightings); merge behavior when a reloaded map conflicts with fresh observation is undefined (BinTrack found *no benefit* from cross-trajectory merging; OVAL clears memory on scene change — do not assume merging is free; define it).

# Design pressure

**Change:**
- **C-3 / E-2: kill the POI short-circuit** — disable `PlaceGrounder` under T1 and in all E-2 runs, with a RED seed. (F1; recon_groundtruth GT-9.)
- **C-3: pre-register the shadow-mode pass criteria** — per-class numeric thresholds and a divergence severity taxonomy (admission/refusal flips as hard gates), and raise T1-only closed-loop missions from 1 to >=3. (F4; AV shadow-testing + behavior-cloning OOD evidence.)
- **C-2: version the embeddings** — per-entry model-id/revision/dim/preprocessing + bounded source crops for re-embedding; version-mismatch degrades to the label/text channel, never cross-space cosine. (F3; vector-DB migration consensus, Drift-Adapter.)
- **C-2: class-conditional persistence** (anchor/volatile) and retrieval-exclusion semantics for decay-marked entries. (F5; DualMap's measured static-vs-dynamic gap.)
- **PG-3 consumption in C-3: re-calibrate all four signals on textured renders** before any T1 admission uses them; treat every pre-W-1 threshold as provisional. (F2; the synthesis's own "no number from this world means anything.")

**Keep:**
- **OWLv2 + the PG-1 GPU path** — every open-license 2026 alternative loses on license (YOLOE/YOLO-World AGPL), latency/VRAM shape (LLMDet/GDINO family), or access (DINO-X, GDINO-1.5+); and the bench proved the world was the blocker for all detectors equally. (F9.)
- **Detector-label-primary retrieval and the Narnia refusal property** — the modality gap makes absolute image-text cosine unusable for abstention; the refusal-first stance remains ahead of the published field. (Q2; res_mapping §5.)
- **Shadow mode itself, T0-byte-identical discipline, pass^3 + null controls, claim scoping in E-2** — all consistent with eval best practice; the fixes above are additions, not replacements.
- **W-1's measured, pre-registered acceptance including the person-recall target** — it is the right world-quality probe even though nothing consumes person detections yet. (F8.)

**Add:**
- **Red-team props in W-1** (person poster, scene-text/storefront decal) as E-2 null-control assets — the only way the plan can exercise map poisoning at all; plus a **fifth abstention signal**: class-conditional metric size + depth-planarity gate from `metric_localizer` output. (F5; AgentPoison, CHAI/SceneTAP, RGB-depth consistency literature.)
- **E-2 controls from its own brief:** blind-baseline row, synonym-phrasing band (OVON tiers), one lighting cell, Wilson CIs; declare texture-pool overlap between dev and held-out scenes; add the recolor shortcut probe as a follow-on card. (F7; OVON 12–23-point phrasing effect, OSMa-Bench 27.9→14.8.)
- **One `DriftingOdomProvider` sensitivity cell in C-2's live proof** and an "oracle-pose-conditioned" stamp on all map/persistence claims. (F6; VLMaps drift failure, 67.4→45.3 outdoor revisit.)
- **A VLM naming lane, provisional-promotion only** — idle-time batch naming with `vlm_proposed` provenance, promoted after k independent-visit confirmations through PG-3; small VLM on demand, never the 8B resident during patrol. (Q3; POPE ~82%, contention 1.54x.)
- **An explicit out-of-scope note + follow-on card stub for the person-channel cutover** (GT-6/GT-7). (F8.)
- **Card ownership for GT-14's `SCENE_HONESTY_NOTE` and the `PerceptionContract` assertion** in C-3's checklist. (F12.)

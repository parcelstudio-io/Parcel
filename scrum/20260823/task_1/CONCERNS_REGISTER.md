## How Claude/Fable must use this register

Account for every concern ID in `FABLE_VERDICT.md`. A compact family/range row
may cover multiple IDs when the disposition and rationale are identical;
exceptions must be listed individually. Detailed reproduction/evidence is
required for `BLOCKER`/`HIGH`; `MEDIUM` and `WATCH/PRESERVE` rows may use a
checked family disposition unless Fable disagrees or finds new evidence:

- `CONFIRM_OPEN` — evidence supports the concern;
- `PROVISIONALLY_CLOSED_IN_DIRTY_TREE` — code appears to address it but is not
  integrated or proved;
- `REFUTE_WITH_EVIDENCE` — name the exact product path, test/evidence, and
  falsifier;
- `DEFER_ACCEPTED_RISK` — name owner, trigger, expiry/review date, and why it
  does not block the next milestone;
- `DUPLICATE_OF <ID>` — merge without losing its evidence or consequence;
- `PRESERVE` — the named property is intentionally retained.

Disposition is not closure. `CONFIRM_OPEN` and provisional blockers remain
open. The Fable verdict records X11 revised and an X12 decision; its supplement
dispositions several exact-delta findings but remains incomplete under the
README reconciliation. Each other physical blocker gates its affected
milestone. Fable
may identify a dependency-safe tranche for consideration, but only the owner
may authorize its spend/dispatch.

One concern does **not** imply one follow-up card. Claude should batch concerns
by boundary and cut a card only when it closes a physical blocker, authority or
safety defect, missing product-path seam, reproducible quality gap, or a
measurable engineering bottleneck. Documentation/style/history findings should
normally be consolidated, archived, linted, or accepted—not multiplied into a
card chain.

Do not create one reviewer/subagent per ID. The canonical coverage partition is
complete and non-overlapping; thematic cross-references inside rows do not
change the owning family. Fable may disposition each range compactly and list
only blocker/high disagreements or different dispositions as exceptions:

| Canonical review family | Exactly owned IDs |
|---|---|
| executive blockers/design decisions | X01–X16 |
| architecture/maintainability | A01–A24 |
| physical robotics/autonomy | R01–R35 |

Severity:

- `BLOCKER`: stops physical motion, prototype promotion, or safe cutover;
- `HIGH`: credible safety, correctness, or maintainability failure;
- `MEDIUM`: material cost/reliability/review burden, but not an immediate stop;
- `WATCH`: monitor or preserve; do not churn without evidence.

## What this register does not prove

The register began as a static risk inventory over a moving shared tree; its
Wave 3 facts are now regenerated against the exact integrated delta. It does
not prove the proposed solution is optimal, the landing's gate result, or that
an absent concern is safe. Claude/Fable must reproduce high-severity evidence
at the reviewed commit, record refutations rather than silently deleting rows,
and keep hardware/target claims at their actual evidence rung.

# Endpoint-policy verdict

**Do not change the production confidence threshold or silence timeout from
this experiment. Do not enable provisional motion admission.**

No one-dimensional confidence/silence pair in the declared grid satisfies
both semantic integrity and latency, and the direct replay does not reproduce
the corrected virtual rig. A 200 ms acknowledgement can also survive a
premature commit and be contradicted when the owner resumes speaking.

The next admissible experiment is a frozen one-shot holdout with real speakers,
unseen utterances and pause lengths, phase/SNR/room/AEC perturbations, exact
commit clocks, ASR transcript completeness, and a real cancelable response
path. Irreversible navigation must remain gated on stable completion; any local
early acknowledgement must be non-authoritative and cancelable. Mounted audio
and independent STOP evidence are still required after the holdout.

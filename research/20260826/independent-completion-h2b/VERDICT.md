versus 114/120 required. It remains default-disabled, isolated from the

## What is supported

The factorization itself is a strong fail-closed result in this synthetic
holdout:

  stale and inconsistent resets;

  outside-boundary claim; and

  uncertainty with zero silent outcomes in the canonical runs.
- goal-ID/nonce and goal-start binding prevents cross-goal and pre-goal replay,
  while cached pose verification is revalidated every step.

  reset and geometry records under exact provider/verifier IDs; an enabled
  latch refuses anything short of three distinct commissioned channels.

Across 360 false-completion opportunities H2b produced zero false claims,
while nominal completion was 120/120. Its positive output remains only a
terminal-claim proposal and never authorizes motion.

## Recommendation

Do not lower the recovery gate or change the registered 5% loss branch. Keep
the contract as a non-integrated research seam and make the next experiment a
recorded-sensor replay with bounded active reacquisition: multiple independent
camera views, Mid-360 residuals, IMU pickup/jump evidence and explicit view
coverage. Pre-register retry/view budgets and evaluate false-claim risk jointly
with recovery coverage. A new untouched replay must pass before considering a
shadow pipeline adapter. The current HMAC seam establishes interface
authenticity only: require independent production sensor processes, key custody,
calibration and failure-domain evidence before any authority-bearing
integration. Physical motion remains out of scope.

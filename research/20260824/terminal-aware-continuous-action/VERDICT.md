# Terminal-aware continuous action — verdict

## Verdict: REFUTED, with a stronger replacement hypothesis

The preregistered claim fails. Against a byte-for-behavior valid H3 replay,
the proposal preserved all 16 initiatives and yielded in zero ticks, but it
increased stationary contacts from **316 to 323**, total contacts from **319
to 323**, and contact time from **89.1 s to 244.6 s**. The mandatory TA1 and
TA2 bars fail decisively.

Two seams are nevertheless supported at mechanism level:

- the all-track TTC join sees front, side and rear closure, while the combined
  policy arm observed moving contacts change from 3 to 0;
- a typed terminal can reach home and return an exact-zero command; four of
  four natural returns succeeded and one terminal owner-preemption had a
  separately observed zero-tick dispatched-command yield.

This is a bundled two-component treatment, not a factorial experiment, so the
moving-contact change cannot be attributed independently to the terminal or
the TTC join. Natural-release `release_command` fields are harness-authored
policy metadata, not an independent gateway/actuator witness. They prove
simulator lifecycle intent, not physical authority release.

They are not sufficient together. Braking turns a moving conflict into a
stationary obstacle when people do not avoid, and a return that begins only
after a 240 s outbound budget cannot protect the active phase. An owner/e-stop
preemption must release authority even if the resulting position is awkward;
the initiative cannot secretly keep moving.

## Prototype decision

Do **not** promote self-authored translation or this terminal controller to
the Unitree prototype. Keep proactive travel disabled by default. The result
supports only an implementation-design card for a body-neutral action
lifecycle and dynamic-occupancy planner.

The replacement hypothesis should be:

> A self-authored action admitted only with a reachable safe-hold invariant,
> an outbound success condition, and reserved return/yield budget, executed by
> a receding-horizon static-plus-dynamic planner, can retain useful progress
> while reaching a safe terminal under noisy and delayed tracks.

Its promotion bar must include zero contacts, bounded clearance, safe-hold
occupancy, useful progress, one-tick authority release, and track
delay/dropout/velocity-noise refuters. `STAND_ASIDE` remains untested here.

This research is still directly valuable: it rules out the tempting but
unsafe design of attaching a return leg and a wider collision detector to the
existing clock-budgeted errand and calling continuous autonomy solved.

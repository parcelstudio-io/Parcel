# EVENT-BUDGET — VERDICT · 2026-08-24

## Verdict: CONFIRMED for the architecture; production enforcement unproven

All preregistered rows pass. Parcel can sustain continuously running local
sensing, safety, drive, memory-state and body-intent loops while reserving
well over half of a $200 monthly API ceiling. The confirmed rule is:

> **Clock-driven local life; event-driven hosted cognition.**

The result does not authorize a cloud inner-monologue loop. The 1 Hz refuter
costs $777.60/month, while ungated ambient speech costs about $571/month.
Conversely, nominal use is $30.72/month p95 and the 500-turn/day case is
$76.95/month p95 under the frozen assumptions.

## Architecture consequence

- Run safety, localization, tracking, novelty, drives, initiative admission,
  gaze/posture and control locally at their required rates.
- Open hosted audio only after the engagement/identity/privacy gate admits a
  turn. Close or suppress it outside an active exchange.
- Invoke the structured online planner only for a committed compound task or
  a local executive's explicit escalation—not every control tick.
- Consolidate memory in bounded batches. Retrieval and ordinary use remain
  local.
- Keep proactive behavior selection local. A hosted model may phrase an
  already-admitted utterance, but cannot own timing, safety or motion.
- Warn at $150 projected/month and deny nonessential hosted calls at $200.
  STOP, HOLD, navigation safety and local social cues must still work.

## Required product work before relying on this verdict

The present ledger and rate card are not yet a trustworthy hard ceiling.
Activation is not wired as a pre-cloud product gate, incomplete provider
events can be uncounted, and already-open sessions can continue spending.
The product therefore needs one fail-closed `HostedCallGovernor` in front of
every provider lane, backed by provider usage reconciliation, per-capability
daily quotas and an offline/local degradation policy. VOICE-GATE still must
prove its mounted false-open and owner-turn bars.

No further local-model size comparison is warranted for this decision.

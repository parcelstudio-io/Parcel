# Sim-plan-1

A deterministic, proposal-only comparison of Parcel's bounded affordance
planner with a fixed-template baseline on 29 authored held-out compositional
missions.

- Method and frozen gates: `DESIGN.md`
- Results and limitations: `RESULTS.md`
- Independent replay and product-path verdict: `VERDICT.md`
- Frozen fixture matrix: `fixtures.json`
- Runner: `experiment.py`
- Canonical evidence: `results.json`
- Integrity report: `verification.json`

Evidence class: **authored symbolic shadow only**. No physics simulator,
hardware, authenticated commissioning, or motion was used.

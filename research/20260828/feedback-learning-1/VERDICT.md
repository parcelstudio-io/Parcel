## What this means for the program

1. **Learn owner taste with a table, not a policy.** The per-category Beta
   table (a) equals or beats the meta-learned policy, (b) is readable and
   resettable, (c) reaches the regret bar. The sequence model's job is the
   *shape* of behaviour (BM-1); the owner's taste lives in counts.
2. **Twenty-five clean jokes is the real budget** — and only if the reward
   is clean. The household will not supply a clean reward: HS-1 measured a
   fold-5 miss rate of 0.375 and the gap sweep's ego-noise numbers say a
   walking dog hears 72–79 dBA of its own feet. Under FL-1's milder noise
   model the bandit never reached the bar in 60 jokes.
3. **Self-echo masking is the design bug to kill first.** With m = 0.3 the
   learner is biased against chuckling; the fix is architectural — do not
   open the reward window until the dog's own audio has ended *and* the
   local lane has AEC (it has none today; `AecStage` is unwired) — not
   statistical.
4. **Look-back latency is easy**: one to six losses. Ship it as an
   executive config value learned by a 4-arm Thompson bandit.
5. **Explicit feedback must be a soft prior, not a gag order.**
6. **Online gradient learning stays off the table** for this household
   mechanism: it lost on every objective it was meant to win and the
   "safety regression" question is still open because 60 tiny updates
   cannot answer it.

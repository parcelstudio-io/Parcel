# Gateway TTL and latency derivation — N24 / RC-4

**Frozen:** 2026-08-16 for the bounded fake/process slice. This reconciles the
accepted Wave-0 targets with the existing control constant before the V1 DTO
duration bound is frozen. It is a derivation record, not a hardware result or a
hazard-analysis substitute.

## Fixed inputs

- Existing control rate: `50 Hz`, hence a `20 ms` period.
- Existing command duration TTL: `command_timeout_s = 0.35 s`, hence `350 ms`
  or `17.5` control periods. The same value is pinned in `ControlTiming`, the
  `control.factory._timing` default, and canonical `configs/robot.yaml`.
- Proposed pilot ceiling range from the accepted plan: `0.3–0.5 m/s`.
- Distance below is the simple upper-bound product `speed × duration`; it is
  not measured braking distance.

settling rather than inherit `0.08/0.12` uncritically.

## Reconciliation

The `350 ms` TTL cannot by itself prove a `150 ms` client/lease-loss stop-
initiation target. The fake process therefore detects local socket loss and
fake lease loss directly and calls `StopMove` without waiting for TTL; the TTL
is only a fallback. Likewise, a `100 ms` sensor-invalidation target says when
positive upstream authority becomes invalid. If an upstream producer were
allowed to keep refreshing until that bound, its last `350 ms` duration could
outlive the invalidation target. N43 must prove the complete chain from source
expiry to no positive refresh and gateway stop.

`StopMove` initiation, RPC completion, fake feedback-confirmed stillness, and
physical motion ended are four different events. N24's ACK is explicitly
admission-only, and its stop report does not confirm stillness unless a
successful fake `StopMove` is followed by a fresh later stationary fake-state
sequence. Only B16 can measure real stop initiation, settling time, distance,
and target-compute scheduling tails.

## Decision

N24 does **not** retune `command_timeout_s`. Changing `0.35 s` would move the
shipping simulator behavior to satisfy proposed targets that do not yet have a
ratified hazard/ODD derivation. If B16 measurement shows the TTL and physical
stop envelope are irreconcilable, that is a stop-and-review outcome, not a
quiet constant change.
